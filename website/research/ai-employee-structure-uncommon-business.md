## Executive Summary

The Uncommon Business, founded by Callan Faulkner, pioneered a model for running lean businesses at scale using "AI employees" -- specialized AI systems that replace or augment traditional human roles across every department. Callan built a $4M business in 18 months with just 4 human employees (all with management/leadership ability), supported by 50+ AI employees. Her framework, **The Uncommon Method**, has been deployed across 1,000+ businesses in 20+ industries.

The core philosophy: **every human on the team manages and trains AI employees.** Before posting a job, the first question is whether an AI agent could do it. The org chart has agents on it.

---

## The Uncommon Method Framework

### Three Core Pillars

1. **Spot Leverage** -- Identify where AI can replace or augment human effort across your business
2. **Build Assets** -- Create reusable AI systems that operate like expert employees
3. **Train Systems** -- Apply human training methodologies to AI, treating each system as an employee that needs onboarding, skill development, and performance management

These three skills are described as what separates $500K founders from $5M founders.

### Key Principle: AI-First Hiring

- Every human employee must have management or leadership ability
- Humans don't do the work themselves -- they train, deploy, and retrain AI employees
- This fundamentally changes hiring criteria and interview processes
- The question before any hire: "Could an agent do this first?"

1. **Flat human layer**: Very few humans (4 in Callan's case), all at leadership level

## The Uncommon Method: How AI Employees Are Built

### Training Methodology

Callan's AI employee training is rooted in **actual human training methodologies** applied to AI:

1. **Knowledge Transfer** -- Feed the AI system with all relevant business knowledge (brand voice, processes, SOPs, success stories, testimonials)
2. **Skill Training** -- Train the AI on specific competencies (e.g., Claude's skill training feature)
3. **Contextual Understanding** -- Ensure the AI understands not just *what* to do but *why* (customer motivations, competitive landscape, brand values)
4. **Continuous Retraining** -- Humans regularly update and retrain AI employees as the business evolves

### Building Process

1. Identify the business function that needs automation
2. Document the SOPs and processes for that function
3. Select the right AI platform (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity)
4. Create the AI system with proper context and training data
5. Connect to business tools via Zapier automations
6. Deploy and monitor performance
7. Retrain and optimize based on results

## Key Takeaways for Our Business

1. **AI employees are not chatbots** -- they are trained, specialized systems built on platforms like Claude Projects with deep business context
2. **The org chart should include AI** -- treat AI systems as real team members with defined roles, responsibilities, and performance expectations
3. **Humans become AI managers** -- the role of every human shifts from "doing the work" to "training and managing AI employees that do the work"
4. **Start with high-leverage roles** -- Brand Guardian (marketing), Sales Wizard (sales), and Deep Research (strategy) appear to be the highest-impact starting points
5. **Zapier is the connective tissue** -- AI employees become truly powerful when connected to business tools via automation platforms
6. **125+ proven prompts** -- Callan provides copy-paste prompts for every business function, suggesting that prompt engineering at scale is a core competency
7. **Continuous retraining is essential** -- AI employees need ongoing updates just like human employees need ongoing training
